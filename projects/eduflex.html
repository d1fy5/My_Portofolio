Case Study

Problem & Solution

The challenge behind the design and how I approached it.

Problem

Too much choice, too little focus

E-learning platforms often overload users — crowded course lists, unclear progress and scattered pages make it hard to find the right course and actually finish it.

Solution

A guided, five-step journey

EduFlex narrows the experience into clear steps: sign up, get matched, explore, learn and track. A clean, blue-toned system with obvious next actions keeps every page focused and guided.

Design Goals

Goals

The core objectives that shaped every screen.

01

Guided Onboarding

Move users from sign-up to a personalized course in under three minutes.

02

Clear Navigation

Reduce options per screen so the primary action is always obvious.

03

Progress Visibility

Let learners see where they are and what comes next at a glance.

04

Focused Learning

Remove distractions inside the lesson room to support deep work.
