Ivory Bakehouse

Bakery Brand & Website Design

A warm, brand-focused website design for a bakery, built around a cozy home page and an inviting menu experience.

Key Pages

Case Study

Problem & Solution

The challenge behind the design and how I approached it.

Problem

Great baking, quiet branding

The bakery had beautiful product photography but no cohesive digital presence to match it — inconsistent pages, hard-to-read menus and little sense of craft or warmth online.

Solution

A story-first website

A warm, ivory and gold-tinted palette paired with layered serif typography. Clear menu hierarchy and generous whitespace let the baked goods feel warm, premium and approachable.

Design Goals

Goals

The core objectives that shaped every page.

01

Brand Warmth

Make the bakery feel inviting and crafted from the very first scroll.

02

Menu Readability

Structure categories and prices so scanning is effortless.

03

Story-Led Home

Use narrative and imagery to build trust before the menu is shown.

04

Strong Local CTA

Guide visitors to visit, order or contact with minimal friction.
