01 — NutriMate

NutriMate

Nutrition & Healthy Lifestyle Mobile App

A modern nutrition and healthy lifestyle mobile app designed to help users track nutrition, discover meals, and maintain healthier daily habits.

Role UI/UX Designer
Platform Mobile App
Tool Figma

Overview

Project Overview

Role UI/UX Designer — responsible for research, wireframing, visual design, and prototyping.
Timeline 4 Weeks — from initial research to final high-fidelity prototype.
Tools Figma for design, prototyping, and design system management.
Platform iOS & Android — designed as a cross-platform mobile application.

The Problem

What We're Solving

Most nutrition tracking apps are overwhelming, cluttered, and focused solely on calorie counting. Users struggle to maintain healthy habits because existing tools feel like a chore rather than a helpful companion. There's a gap for an app that makes nutrition awareness simple, visual, and enjoyable.

The Goal

What We Set Out To Do

Design a clean, intuitive mobile app that helps users effortlessly track their daily nutrition, discover new healthy meals, and build lasting wellness habits — without the complexity of traditional nutrition trackers.

The Solution

What NutriMate Does

NutriMate simplifies nutrition tracking with a clean, visually-driven interface. Users can log meals in seconds, view clear nutritional breakdowns, discover recipes tailored to their goals, and track daily habits through gentle, motivating feedback loops. The design prioritizes clarity and ease of use, making healthy living feel achievable rather than overwhelming.
