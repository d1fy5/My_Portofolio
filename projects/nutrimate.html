Case Study

Problem & Solution

The challenge behind the design and how I approached it.

Problem

Tracking feels like homework

Nutrition apps often feel heavy — endless logging, dense dashboards and cold data make healthy habits feel like a chore instead of something worth keeping up.

Solution

Effortless, one-tap tracking

NutriMate distills tracking into what matters: pick or scan a meal, see the numbers clearly, and build habits at a glance. A calm, green-toned interface keeps motivation high and friction low.

Design Goals

Goals

The core objectives that shaped every screen.

01

Simplicity First

Make daily logging feel effortless with scan-first flows and one-tap entries.

02

Habit Formation

Encourage consistent use through visible progress and gentle, friendly nudges.

03

Clarity of Data

Present calories and insights in a way users can actually read and act on.

04

Friendly Tone

A warm, approachable visual language that feels encouraging, not clinical.
